6. JAVASCRIPT BEHAVIOR & INTERACTIVE ENGINE (script.js)
================================================================================
Script is initialized inside `DOMContentLoaded` with the following 7 modules:

1. Mobile Navigation Drawer:
   - Elements: #mobileMenuToggle, #mobileNavDrawer, #mobileNavClose, #drawerOverlay
   - Toggles .open class and manages body overflow-y lock.

2. Search Bar Dropdowns & Counter:
   - Destination Popover: #destField & #destDropdownPopover
   - Travelers Popover: #travelersField & #travelersDropdownPopover
   - Counter buttons (.counter-btn) for Adults and Children.
   - Outside click listener closes all active popovers.

3. Search Action (#searchSubmitBtn):
   - Shows feedback spinner ("Searching..."), sends a luxury toast, and smoothly scrolls to #popular-destinations.

4. Destination Cards Interactive Sync (.dest-card-box):
   - Clicking a card updates search bar's destination text and plays card bounce animation.

5. Expanding Accordion (.accordion-panel):
   - Desktop: 'mouseenter' expands panel (.active class).
   - Mobile/Desktop: 'click' activates panel.

6. Quick Search Modal & Tags:
   - #quickSearchModal, opens with shortcut or button, closes on ESC or click-outside.

7. Mobile Testimonials Swipe & Dot Sync:
   - Listens to horizontal scroll on .testimonials-grid.
   - Computes center card offset and dynamically toggles .t-dot.active.
   - Clicking any dot scrolls the corresponding card into view smoothly.

8. About Page Pillars Carousel Swipe & Dot Sync:
   - Listens to horizontal scroll on .pillars-grid.
   - Computes center card offset and dynamically toggles .p-dot.active.
   - Clicking any dot scrolls the corresponding pillar card into view smoothly.

9. Luxury Toast Notification:
   - `showNotification(msg)`: Spawns a floating pill toast with gold star icon ("✦") at the bottom of the screen.
