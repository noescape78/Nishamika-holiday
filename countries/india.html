Spiritual & Cultural Capital

Varanasi

Experience the ancient heartbeat of India along the sacred Ganges. Witness evening Ganga Aarti ceremonies by private boat, historic ghat walks, and centuries-old silk weaving traditions.

Private Boat Aarti Kashi Vishwanath Heritage Ghats Walk
Royal Fortresses & Palaces

Jaipur & Rajasthan

The Pink City and beyond—Amber Fort, opulent royal palace stays, vibrant textile bazaars, and golden desert sunsets in Jaisalmer and Udaipur.

Palace Stays Amber Fort Desert Sundowner

Kerala Backwaters

Glide through tranquil emerald waterways on a luxury private houseboat, unwind with authentic Ayurvedic wellness treatments, and wander through misty tea estates in Munnar.

Luxury Houseboat Ayurveda Wellness Munnar Tea Gardens

Witness the ethereal sunrise over the Taj Mahal, explore the majestic red sandstone ramparts of Agra Fort, and discover the forgotten Mughal capital of Fatehpur Sikri.
