—   OUR PHILOSOPHY   —

Why We Built Nishamika Holiday

When we established Nishamika Holiday, our purpose was clear: to bring genuine hospitality, personal care, and thoughtful curation back to international travel. In an era overrun by impersonal booking algorithms and crowded bus tours, travelers were losing the magic and ease of real exploration.

We believe that a holiday should be effortless from the first conversation. When you trust us with your journey to Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, India, or Nepal, you are not handed off to an anonymous representative. You receive our personal attention, hand-inspected luxury stays, private experienced chauffeurs, and 24/7 on-tour support.

We don’t strive to be the biggest agency. We strive to be the most trusted. Thank you for welcoming us into your journey.

Abhishek Kumar Srivastava, Anamika Gupta & Arun Shrivastava
Founders & Directors, Nishamika Holiday
